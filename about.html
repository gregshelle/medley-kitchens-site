Hi!  I'm Dave;  owner, carpenter, cabinetmaker and labourer for Medley Kitchens. Along with my creative wife Merna and apprentice / son Darren we provide a wide array of home improvement services, specializing in complete kitchen renovations and traditional cabinetry. Our small family business provides a quality assured, full valued, pleasant renovation experience. Here are some reasons to entrust us with your projects.
  • Over 30 years experience in many aspects of carpentry, cabinetry and renovation.

  • Licensed General Carpenter since 1990.

  • Extensive experience restoring century homes.

  • Custom designed furniture, wall units, and mill work.

  • Portico and porch design, build and restoration.

  • Interior/Exterior doors, trim, and fireplace mantles.

  • Natural stone and ceramic tile installation.

  • Drywall, painting and decorating.
